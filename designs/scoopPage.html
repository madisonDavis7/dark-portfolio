Scoop & Joy Icecream

Background

I got the idea for this design after seeing a color pallete I liked. I then based the branding, aesthetic, and company around those colors to create a playful and summery website design. This was done in a couple days as a personal project. I wanted to work on my Figma skills and incorporate lots of color and abstract shapes.

Tools and Skills

Figma Iconology Color Theory Abstract Shapes

Key Features

Icecream Shop Branding

Explore our solar system by clicking through the Sun and planets.

Imagery

There is an interactive map integrated into the app that let's user see the current location of the ISS.

Color

The Sun and it's surrounding planets each have 4 facts listed on their respective pages.

Solution

I ended up creating a design with lots of images but an overall minimal feel. I wanted it to be cohesive so I used the same ombre background and stars throughout to have everything feel like it belongs. After finishing the web version I translated the design to fit on a mobile screen as well, making sure eveyrthing was still readable, the essential information was easy found, and the aesthetic matched.
